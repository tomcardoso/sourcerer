Sourcerer

Source management for investigative journalists

All your contacts, projects, and outreach history — encrypted on your own machine. No cloud, no accounts, no telemetry. Built for reporters who need to protect their sources. Unlock with a master password; everything else stays local.

Contacts view

Features

Encrypted at rest

AES-256 via SQLCipher. The key is derived fresh on each unlock with Argon2id and never written to disk.

Project workspaces

Organise sources into local or shared projects with per-contact status, priority, and reporter attribution.

Outreach tracking

Log every call, email, and meeting. Configurable reminders and staleness indicators keep nothing slipping.

Deduplication

Fuzzy name matching and exact email/phone signals surface duplicate contacts for one-click side-by-side merging.

Chrome extension

Capture full-page screenshots from any tab and link them to a source record, stored encrypted alongside the database.

Import & export

Import from CSV with semicolon multi-value fields. Export to CSV, Excel, or vCard — with an optional sanitised mode.
